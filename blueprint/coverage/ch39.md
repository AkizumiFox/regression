# Coverage checklist — Chapter 39, GLMs in Practice; the Bayesian Thread

Built from: Fahrmeir et al. (2021) §5.3 (*Models for Nonnegative Continuous Response
Variables*), §5.6 (*Bayesian Generalized Linear Models*, with 5.6.1–5.6.3), §5.7
(*Regularization and Boosting in GLMs*), §5.8 (bibliographic notes), and §6.1, §6.4–6.5 of
chapter 6 (*Categorical Regression Models*); Agresti (2015) chapter 10, *Bayesian Linear and
Generalized Linear Modeling* (§10.1 the Bayesian approach, §10.2 Bayesian linear models,
§10.3 Bayesian GLMs with 10.3.1–10.3.3, §10.4 empirical and hierarchical Bayes, chapter notes
10.1–10.11); Rencher and Schaalje (2008) chapter 11, *Multiple Regression: Bayesian Inference*
(§11.2–11.5). Agresti's chapter has no text layer in the scanned copy, so its items were read
from the rendered pages and completed from the standard literature (Gelman et al. 2013;
Tierney and Kadane 1986; Albert and Chib 1993; Gelman et al. 2008).

Items list *topics and results*, not wording. The chapter teaches each one in its own words,
with its own proofs, examples and numbers. Items covered by earlier chapters are marked with
the label that covers them.

Legend: F = Fahrmeir et al., A = Agresti, R = Rencher and Schaalje. "→" = where it is covered.
