# Coverage checklist — Chapter 43, Smoothing: Kernels, Splines and Penalties

Built from Fahrmeir, Kneib, Lang and Marx 2e §8.1 and §8.3 (the planning source F8; its
§8.2, bivariate and spatial smoothing, belongs to chapter 44), together with the standard
literature the chapter is answerable to: Wahba (*Spline Models for Observational Data*,
1990), Green and Silverman (*Nonparametric Regression and Generalized Linear Models*,
1994), Fan and Gijbels (*Local Polynomial Modelling and Its Applications*, 1996), Wand and
Jones (*Kernel Smoothing*, 1995), de Boor (*A Practical Guide to Splines*, rev. ed. 2001),
Ruppert, Wand and Carroll (*Semiparametric Regression*, 2003), Eilers and Marx (1996) and
Hastie, Tibshirani and Friedman (*The Elements of Statistical Learning*, 2e). Items list
*topics and results*, not wording; the chapter teaches each one in its own words, with its
own proofs, examples and numbers. Items covered by earlier chapters are marked with the
label that covers them.

Legend: F = Fahrmeir et al. §8, W = Wahba, GS = Green and Silverman, FG = Fan and Gijbels,
WJ = Wand and Jones, dB = de Boor, RWC = Ruppert, Wand and Carroll, EM96 = Eilers and Marx,
ESL = Hastie, Tibshirani and Friedman. "→" = where it is covered in ch. 43.
