# Coverage checklist — Chapter 40, GLMMs and Generalized Estimating Equations

Built from: Agresti (2015) chapter 9, *Modeling Correlated Responses*, §9.4–9.6 (marginal
models and GEE, generalized linear mixed models, multilevel models) — the scanned pages of that
chapter carry no text layer, so the items below were assembled from the standard topics of the
literature rather than from the file; Fahrmeir et al. (2021) §7.5–7.6 (*Generalized Linear Mixed
Models*, *Likelihood and Bayesian Inference in GLMMs*). Items list *topics and results*, not
wording. The chapter teaches each one in its own words, with its own proofs, examples and
numbers. Items covered by earlier chapters are marked with the label that covers them.

Legend: A = Agresti, F = Fahrmeir et al., L = standard literature (Liang & Zeger 1986,
Breslow & Clayton 1993, Zeger, Liang & Albert 1988, Pepe & Anderson 1994, Pan 2001, …).
"→" = where it is covered in ch. 40.
