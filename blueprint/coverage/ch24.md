# Coverage checklist — Chapter 24, Errors in Variables

Built from: Seber and Lee 2e §9.6 (Effect of random explanatory variables: random regressors without error,
fixed regressors measured with error, round-off errors, working rules, random regressors measured with error,
Berkson's model), the only planning source with any coverage. This is a new chapter, so the rest of the checklist is
the standard content of the measurement-error and instrumental-variables literature (Fuller 1987; Carroll, Ruppert,
Stefanski and Crainiceanu 2006; Cook and Stefanski 1994; Wright 1928; Anderson and Rubin 1949; Staiger and Stock
1997). Items list *topics and results*, not wording. All prose, proofs, examples, data (simulated) and exercises are
original.

Legend: S = Seber and Lee, L = standard literature. "→" = where it is covered (labels are in
`src/ch24-errors-in-variables/`; earlier chapters' labels are cited as such).
