# Coverage checklist — Chapter 23, Bootstrap and Permutation Inference

New chapter. The only planning-source coverage is Sen & Srivastava (1990) §5.4 (a pointer: residual
bootstrap for a linear hypothesis). The rest of the checklist is built from the standard literature
(Efron 1979; Freedman 1981; Wu 1986; Liu 1988; Mammen 1993; Hall 1988, 1992; Efron 1987; Davison and
Hinkley 1997; Freedman and Lane 1983; ter Braak 1992; Kennedy 1995; Anderson and Legendre 1999; Anderson
and Robinson 2001; DiCiccio and Romano 2017; Athreya 1987; Künsch 1989). Items list topics and results,
not wording. "→" = where the chapter covers it.
