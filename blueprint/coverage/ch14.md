# Coverage checklist — Chapter 14, Correlation, Lack of Fit and Prediction Theory

Built from: Christensen 5e §6.3–6.7 (general prediction theory, multiple correlation, partial
correlation, best linear unbiased prediction, testing lack of fit); Rencher & Schaalje 2e ch. 10
(multiple regression with random x's); Agresti (2015) §3.3–3.4 (scanned pages 96–107: confidence and
prediction intervals, worked example with model checking and partial correlation).
Items list *topics and results*, not wording. The chapter teaches each one in its own words, with its
own proofs, examples and numbers. Last column: where it is covered.

Legend: C = Christensen, R = Rencher & Schaalje, A = Agresti.
