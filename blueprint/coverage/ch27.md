# Coverage checklist — Chapter 27, Shrinkage: Ridge, Principal Components and the Lasso

Built from: Christensen 5e §13.3–13.5 (canonical form, principal component regression, generalized
inverse regression, classical and generalized ridge, ridge on principal components, more on mean
squared error); Sen & Srivastava ch. 12 (principal component regression, ridge regression, the
shrinkage estimator); Fahrmeir et al. 2e §4.2 (statistical regularization, ridge, the lasso,
geometry of regularized estimates, partial regularization). Items list *topics and results*, not
wording. The chapter teaches each one in its own words, with its own proofs, examples and numbers
(Longley's data, a two-regressor design, constructed simulations; the sources' Coleman report,
supervisor, house-price and used-car examples are not used).

Legend: C = Christensen, N = Sen & Srivastava, F = Fahrmeir et al. "→" = where it is covered
(labels; earlier chapters by their own labels).
