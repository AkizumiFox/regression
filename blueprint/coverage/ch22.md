# Coverage checklist — Chapter 22, Transformations

Built from: Sen & Srivastava (1990) ch. 9 (Transformations, all sections); Seber & Lee 2e §10.3
(Dealing with curvature), with the transformation parts of §10.4.3 (transforming to equalize variances)
and §10.5.2–10.5.3 (transforming the response, transform both sides); Christensen 5e §12.7
(Transformations). Items list *topics and results*, not wording. The chapter teaches each in its own words,
with its own proofs, examples (Engel's 1857 data from statsmodels, public domain; simulations with fixed
seeds; exact probability computations) and exercises.

Legend: N = Sen & Srivastava, S = Seber & Lee, C = Christensen. "→" = where it is covered.
