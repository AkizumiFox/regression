# Coverage checklist — Chapter 44, Additive, Geoadditive and Structured Additive Models

Built from: Fahrmeir, Kneib, Lang and Marx, *Regression: Models, Methods and Applications*
2e, ch. 9 (Structured Additive Regression), §9.1–9.7; Agresti, *Foundations of Linear and
Generalized Linear Models*, §11.3 (the scanned chapter has no text layer, so its items are
taken from the standard content of that section: smoothing in a GLM, backfitting and local
scoring, GAMs for binary and count data). The remaining items come from the standard
literature the chapter is answerable to — Friedman and Stuetzle (1981), Buja, Hastie and
Tibshirani (1989), Hastie and Tibshirani (*Generalized Additive Models*, 1990),
Hastie and Tibshirani (1993, varying coefficients), Besag, York and Mollié (1991),
Kammann and Wand (2003), Fahrmeir, Kneib and Lang (2004), Wood (*Generalized Additive
Models: An Introduction with R*, 2e, 2017), Wood, Pya and Säfken (2016), Wood (2011),
Marra and Wood (2012) and Gu (*Smoothing Spline ANOVA Models*, 2e).

Items list *topics and results*, not wording; the chapter teaches each one in its own
words, with its own proofs, examples and numbers. Items covered by earlier chapters are
marked with the label that covers them.

Legend: F = Fahrmeir et al. ch. 9, A = Agresti §11.3, HT = Hastie and Tibshirani,
W = Wood. "→" = where it is covered in ch. 44.
