# Coverage checklist — Chapter 42, Polynomials, Piecewise Fits and the Road to Splines

Built from: Seber & Lee 2e §7.1–7.3 (polynomial regression, ill-conditioning, orthogonal
polynomials, piecewise polynomial fitting, spline functions, several variables) and §6.5–6.6
(two-phase linear regression, local linear regression); Fahrmeir, Kneib, Lang & Marx 2e §2.5 (simple
nonparametric regression, panorama); plus the standard literature on regression splines
(Schoenberg 1946; Poirier 1973; Wold 1974; Smith 1979; de Boor 2001; Harrell 2015).
Items list *topics and results*, not wording. The chapter teaches each one in its own words,
with its own proofs, examples and numbers. Status column filled after writing.

Legend: S = Seber & Lee, F = Fahrmeir et al., L = standard literature (no source-book
section). "→" = where it is covered.
