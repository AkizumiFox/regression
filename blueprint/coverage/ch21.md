# Coverage checklist — Chapter 21, Non-Normality, Heteroscedasticity and Serial Correlation

Built from: Sen & Srivastava ch. 5 (normality assumption), ch. 6 (unequal variances), ch. 7 §7.1–7.3 and
§7.6 (generalized and estimated GLS, serial correlation, Durbin–Watson); Seber & Lee 2e §10.4–10.5
(nonconstant variance and serial correlation, departures from normality); Christensen 5e §12.2–12.4
(checking normality, independence, heteroscedasticity, sandwich estimators).
Items list *topics and results*, not wording. The chapter teaches each one in its own words, with its
own proofs, examples and numbers. Last column: where it is covered.

Legend: N = Sen & Srivastava, S = Seber & Lee, C = Christensen.
