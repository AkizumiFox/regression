# Coverage checklist — Chapter 45, Quantile Regression and GAMLSS

Built from: Fahrmeir, Kneib, Lang and Marx 2e, chapter 10 (Distributional Regression
Models), the only planning source that treats the topic. Because chapter 10 is a
survey, the checklist is completed from the standard literature the chapter is
answerable to — Koenker and Bassett (1978), Koenker (*Quantile Regression*, 2005),
Newey and Powell (1987), Efron (1991), Jones (1994), Rigby and Stasinopoulos (2005),
Cole and Green (1992), Gneiting and Raftery (2007) and Gneiting, Balabdaoui and
Raftery (2007). Items list *topics and results*, not wording; the chapter teaches
each one in its own words, with its own proofs, examples and numbers. Items covered
by earlier chapters are marked with the label that covers them.

Legend: F = Fahrmeir et al. ch. 10, KB = Koenker and Bassett, K = Koenker (2005),
NP = Newey and Powell, RS = Rigby and Stasinopoulos, GR = Gneiting and Raftery.
"→" = where it is covered in ch. 45.
