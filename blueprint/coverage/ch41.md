# Coverage checklist — Chapter 41, Missing Data

A **new chapter**: none of the six planning sources develops the topic. Christensen 5e
§9.3 only points at it, and its §9.4 (missing observations in a designed experiment) is
covered by chapter 18. The checklist below is therefore built from the standard
literature the chapter is answerable to — Rubin (1976), Little and Rubin
(*Statistical Analysis with Missing Data*, 3e), Rubin (*Multiple Imputation for
Nonresponse in Surveys*, 1987), Schafer (*Analysis of Incomplete Multivariate Data*,
1997), Dempster, Laird and Rubin (1977), McLachlan and Krishnan (*The EM Algorithm and
Extensions*, 2e), van Buuren (*Flexible Imputation of Missing Data*, 2e) and the
National Research Council (2010) report. Items list *topics and results*, not wording;
the chapter teaches each one in its own words, with its own proofs, examples and
numbers. Items covered by earlier chapters are marked with the label that covers them.

Legend: LR = Little and Rubin, R76/R87 = Rubin, S = Schafer, DLR = Dempster, Laird and
Rubin, MK = McLachlan and Krishnan, vB = van Buuren, NRC = National Research Council.
"→" = where it is covered in ch. 41.
