# Coverage checklist — Chapter 28, High-Dimensional Regression: p > n

New chapter. None of the six planning sources treats p > n in depth; Agresti (2015) §11.2 mentions it
briefly (through a Bayesian prior) and is cited in the Notes, not used as a source. The checklist is built
from the standard literature: Tibshirani (1996); Greenshtein and Ritov (2004); Zhao and Yu (2006);
Meinshausen and Bühlmann (2006, 2010); Candès and Tao (2007); Bickel, Ritov and Tsybakov (2009); van de Geer
and Bühlmann (2009); Wainwright (2009); Raskutti, Wainwright and Yu (2010, 2011); Bühlmann and van de Geer
(2011); Zhang and Zhang (2014); van de Geer, Bühlmann, Ritov and Dezeure (2014); Javanmard and Montanari
(2014); Leeb and Pötscher (2005, 2006); Belkin et al. (2019); Belkin, Hsu and Xu (2020); Bartlett et al. (2020);
Hastie, Montanari, Rosset and Tibshirani (2022). Items list topics and results, not wording.
"→" = where the chapter covers it (by label).
