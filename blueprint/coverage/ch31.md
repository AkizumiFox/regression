# Coverage checklist — Chapter 31, The General Gauss–Markov Model

Built from: Christensen 5e ch. 10 (General Gauss–Markov models, §10.1–10.5) and §2.7
(generalized least squares); Seber & Lee 2e §3.10; Rencher & Schaalje 2e §7.8; plus the
standard results on feasible generalized least squares from the econometric and
variance-function literature (Aitken 1935; Zyskind 1967; Rao 1967, 1971, 1973; Kruskal 1968;
Watson 1955; Bloomfield & Watson 1975; Knott 1975; Amemiya 1985; Carroll & Ruppert 1988).
Items list *topics and results*, not wording. The chapter teaches each one in its own words,
with its own proofs, examples and numbers.

Legend: C = Christensen, S = Seber & Lee, R = Rencher & Schaalje, L = standard literature.
"→" = where it is covered; a label from an earlier chapter means that chapter already has it
and Chapter 31 only cites it.
