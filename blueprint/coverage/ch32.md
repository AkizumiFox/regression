# Coverage checklist — Chapter 32, Random Effects and Linear Mixed Models

Built from: Rencher & Schaalje 2e ch. 17 (Linear Mixed Models); Agresti (2015) §9.2–9.3
(Normal Linear Mixed Models; Fitting and Prediction) — the scanned pages carry no text layer,
so the items from that source are taken from the standard content of those sections;
Fahrmeir, Kneib, Lang & Marx 2e §7.1–7.4 (Linear Mixed Models for Longitudinal and Clustered
Data; General Linear Mixed Model; Likelihood Inference; Gibbs Sampler); Sen & Srivastava
§7.4–7.5 (Nested Errors; the Growth Curve Model). Items list *topics and results*, not
wording. The chapter teaches each one in its own words, with its own proofs, examples and
numbers (a simulated twelve-laboratory proficiency study with stated parameters and a fixed
seed, and Grunfeld's investment panel from statsmodels, public domain; the sources' examples
and data are not used).

Legend: R = Rencher & Schaalje, A = Agresti, F = Fahrmeir et al., N = Sen & Srivastava.
"→" = where it is covered in ch. 32 (labels), or the earlier chapter that already covers it.
